Fix Interview Ace question generation.

The AI interviewer must NEVER assume the candidate has banking, operations, customer-service, or any other experience unless the candidate profile or previous answer explicitly confirms it.

Questions must be generated dynamically from:

1. Selected job role
2. Industry
3. Experience level
4. Job description, when available
5. Candidate's actual profile
6. Previous answers

For example, if the selected role is **Sales Executive**, questions must primarily assess:

* Prospecting
* Lead generation
* Customer discovery
* Product pitching
* Objection handling
* Negotiation
* Closing
* Target achievement
* Follow-up
* Customer relationship management
* Sales discipline
* CRM usage
* Communication and persuasion

Do NOT ask Sales Executive candidates banking-specific or operations-specific questions unless the selected industry/JD explicitly requires them.

If the selected role is Sales Manager, assess leadership, coaching, targets, pipeline, forecasting, performance management and sales strategy.

If the selected role is Operations Executive, assess process management, accuracy, coordination, SLA/KPI handling and operational problem-solving.

Create separate role-specific question frameworks rather than one generic interview template.

The interviewer must also distinguish:

* Candidate-provided experience
* Job-required experience
* Assumptions

Never convert an assumption into a fact.

Before generating each question, internally validate:

“Is this question relevant to the selected role, industry and experience level?”

If not, reject it and generate another question.

Questions should progressively adapt based on previous answers rather than following a fixed sequence.

Do not claim this is fixed until you test at least:
Sales Executive, Sales Manager, Operations Executive and Customer Service Executive with different experience levels and verify that the questions genuinely change according to role and experience.
