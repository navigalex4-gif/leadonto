# Lead Onto — English Guru: Landing Page Redesign & Growth Strategy

> Built for cold Google Ads traffic (India). Goal: sign-ups → paid → daily habit → defensible moat.

---

## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — before you spend one rupee on ads

The new page (`index.html`) contains **placeholder proof** (testimonials, learner counts, ratings).
Every element marked `data-placeholder="true"` or wrapped in a `<!-- ⚠️ REPLACE -->` comment
**must be replaced with real, verifiable data before the page goes live.**

Google Ads suspends accounts for unverifiable claims, and fake testimonials are actionable under
India's Consumer Protection Act (misleading advertisement). If you don't have 10,000 learners yet,
say "Join our first 500 learners." Honest small numbers convert better than unbelievable big ones.

## 1. What is broken today

The screenshot shows the **logged-in application**, not a landing page. A cold visitor who clicks
your ad lands inside a dashboard. Here is what that costs you:

| # | Leak | Why it kills conversion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No headline, no value proposition** | The visitor has 3–5 seconds to answer "what is this and why should I care?" The page never tells them. |
| 2 | **App chrome shown to strangers** | `Fluency Suite ▾ · Career Suite ▾ · Progress · Saved · B2B · Admin · 999999 · Sign out` — eight exits above the fold. Every nav link is a leak. `Admin` and `Sign out` on a public page signal "broken / not for me". |
| 3 | **Money before value** | "Uses **1 credit per 12 minutes** (5 credits/hour) · first block charged at start · Balance: 999999 · Top up" is the second thing the eye lands on. You are asking a stranger to think about billing before they've had a single win. |
| 4 | **Buried lede** | The single best thing you have — *15 minutes free, no signup* — appears as grey 12px text far down the page. It should be the loudest promise on the screen. |
| 5 | **Competing CTAs** | `Start Speaking Practice`, `Change Teacher`, and `Live` all fight for the same click. Split attention = no click. |
| 6 | **Broken layout** | A horizontal scrollbar is visible at 1366px. On a ₹9,000 Android phone this page is unusable — and 80%+ of your Google Ads traffic will be mobile. |
| 7 | **Zero trust** | No proof, no pricing, no FAQ, no refund policy, no privacy line, no "cancel anytime". Cold traffic does not hand over a phone number to an unproven page. |
| 8 | **No outcome** | "Start Speaking Practice" is a *feature*. Nobody wants speaking practice. They want the job, the promotion, the confidence to not freeze in a meeting. |
| 9 | **Ad-to-page mismatch** | Your `<title>` is "Speak with Confidence. Prepare for the Role You Want." — generic and dual-purpose (English *and* interviews). Google Quality Score punishes weak message match; you'll pay more per click than competitors. |
| 10 | **Feature-named UI** | "Fluency Suite", "Career Suite" are internal names. Nobody searches for those. |

## 2. Positioning: the one sentence

> **The AI English teacher who switches to your mother tongue the moment you get stuck — and brings you back.**

This is the wedge. Global apps (pronunciation coaches, AI conversation apps) instruct **only in
English**. That is precisely the failure mode for a Tier-2/3 Indian learner: the moment they don't
understand the correction, the session dies and they churn. Your vernacular bridge is not a feature
— it is the entire reason this product exists.

**Do not position as "learn English."** Position as **"stop freezing."** Your buyer already knows
English grammar — twelve years of school taught them. They fail at the moment of speaking. Sell the
moment, not the language.

## 4. The addiction loop

Retention is not a marketing problem, it's a product loop. Ship this loop:

```
TRIGGER   → 8:30pm WhatsApp voice note from "Neha Ma'am":
            "Aaj ka topic: salary negotiation. 5 minute?"
            (WhatsApp, not push — India open rates are far higher)
   ↓
ACTION    → Tap → speaking within 10 seconds. No login screen, no menu.
            One tap = one session. Friction is the enemy.
   ↓
REWARD    → Variable, three layers:
            a) Instant  — live "you sounded confident 👍" during the call
            b) Session  — Fluency Score moves (+2 pronunciation, +1 fluency)
            c) Surprise — random unlock: a new teacher, a bonus credit,
                          a "you used 3 new words today" card
   ↓
INVESTMENT→ The thing they cannot take with them if they leave:
            • Streak (loss aversion)
            • Personal Mistake Book — every error they've ever made,
              auto-collected, spaced-repetition scheduled
            • Fluency Score history graph
            • Shareable certificate for LinkedIn/WhatsApp status
```

**Streak rules that actually work** (Duolingo's hard-won lessons):
- 1 free "streak freeze" per month, and one purchasable — losing a 40-day streak makes people quit forever, not try harder.
- Minimum daily goal must be achievable in **3 minutes**. A 15-minute daily goal produces a 4-day streak; a 3-minute goal produces a 60-day one.
- Show the streak *before* the session, not after.

**Social loop:** weekly "Speaking League" of 20 random peers at the same level. Purely
opt-in-by-default cohort ranking. Costs nothing to build, materially lifts D7.

## 5. Free → paid ladder

The credit model is your friend in India — subscription fear is real. But *credits alone don't
create recurring revenue*. Run both.

| Stage | Offer | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **15 min, no signup, no card** | Ad landing. Value before identity. Already built — just surface it. |
| 1 | **Sign up → 20 free credits (4 hours)** | Price of entry is a phone number, paid *after* they've felt the value |
| 2 | **₹1 for 7 days, unlimited** | The single highest-converting India edtech offer. Also captures a UPI Autopay mandate — the real prize. |
| 3 | **Starter ₹199/mo** — 60 credits (12 hrs) | Entry tier. Beats the global apps on price. |
| 4 | **Fluency Pro ₹399/mo** — unlimited + Mistake Book + Fluency Report | ⭐ Most popular. Anchor everything here. |
| 5 | **Career Pro ₹799/mo** — + mock interviews, resume voice-over, recruiter-shareable score | Where the margin is. Job-seekers pay 4x. |
| 6 | **Annual ₹2,999** (= ₹249/mo) | Cash upfront, kills churn |
| 7 | **B2B seats ₹299/seat/mo** | You already have a `B2B` nav item — that's the real business. BPOs, IT services, retail chains, coaching institutes. |

**Critical mechanics:**
- **UPI Autopay mandate at ₹1**, not at ₹399. Capturing the mandate is the conversion; the price is the trap.
- **Never show a credit balance to a free user before their first session.** Show minutes remaining, not currency.
- **Grace credits on churn:** when someone cancels, gift 30 credits expiring in 7 days. Recovers a meaningful slice.

## 6. The real moat (five layers, in order of defensibility)

Anyone can wrap a speech model. These are the things a funded competitor cannot copy in 12 months:

**1. Indian-English error corpus (data flywheel) — strongest**
Every session produces labelled data on how Indian speakers of *specific* L1s (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu,
Bengali, Marathi…) fail at English. A Tamil speaker's errors are systematically different from a
Bengali speaker's. Log them, cluster by L1, and your corrections get sharper for a Tamil speaker
than any global model can be. Every user makes the product better for the next user of the same
mother tongue. **Global players will never have this data because they don't ask for L1.**

**2. Employer-recognised Fluency Score (two-sided network)**
You already have a `B2B` tab. Turn the Fluency Score into a credential: get 20–50 BPOs, IT staffing
firms and retail chains to accept "Lead Onto Fluency Score ≥ 70" in hiring screens. The moment a job
ad says that, learners *must* come to you. Employers get free screening; you get compulsory demand.
This is the moat that compounds fastest and is nearly impossible to dislodge.

**3. Vernacular teacher personas (brand + switching cost)**
"Neha Ma'am" who speaks Hindi + Bengali is a *relationship*, not a feature. Learners build 60 days of
context with a named teacher who remembers their mistakes. Migrating to a competitor means starting
from zero. Deepen this: the teacher references last week's session by name.

**4. WhatsApp-native distribution**
Practise via WhatsApp voice notes. You own the highest-engagement channel in India, at near-zero CAC
after acquisition, and you become part of an existing habit instead of asking for a new one. Global
competitors are App Store-native and structurally cannot do this well.

**5. Low-bandwidth audio-only mode**
Avatar-based competitors need good bandwidth. An audio-first path that works on patchy 3G unlocks
Tier-3 and rural India — the largest and least contested segment.

**What is NOT a moat:** the AI model, voice quality, the UI, the credit pricing. All copyable in a
sprint. Don't build the marketing around them.

## 7. Google Ads plan

**Never point ads at `/english-guru` (the app).** Point them at dedicated landing URLs.

| Ad group | Intent | Landing URL | Headline must contain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spoken English | "english speaking practice app" | `/lp/speak` | "English Speaking Practice" |
| Interview prep | "hr interview questions english" | `/lp/interview` | "Interview in English" |
| BPO / call centre | "call center english training" | `/lp/bpo` | "Call Centre English" |
| IELTS speaking | "ielts speaking practice" | `/lp/ielts` | "IELTS Speaking" |
| Hindi intent | "english bolna kaise sikhe" | `/lp/hindi` | Devanagari in H1 |

**Rules:**
1. **Message match** — the H1 must repeat the searched keyword near-verbatim. This is the single
   biggest Quality Score lever and directly lowers your CPC.
2. **One conversion action** per page. Everything else is a link, not a button.
3. **Mobile LCP under 2 seconds.** The page ships as a single file with inline CSS and zero
   render-blocking requests specifically for this. Don't add a font CDN or a chat widget.
4. **Sitelinks** to `/pricing`, `/teachers`, `/for-business`, `/free-trial`.
5. **Callouts**: "15 Min Free · No Card", "Speaks 13 Indian Languages", "Cancel Anytime", "UPI Accepted".
6. **Track micro-conversions**, not just signup. The page fires `gtag` events for
   `hero_cta_click`, `demo_language_select`, `pricing_view`, `plan_click`, `faq_open`.
   Optimise the campaign on `trial_start`, not on pageview.
7. **Negative keywords** (essential): `free`, `pdf`, `download`, `crack`, `mod apk`, `job`,
   `vacancy`, `teacher job`, `salary`, `duolingo`.
8. **Landing page compliance:** you need a visible Privacy Policy, Terms, Refund Policy and a
   working contact method, or Google will disapprove. Links are stubbed in the footer — make them real.

**Testing order (highest expected lift first):**
1. Hero headline — Hinglish vs pure English
2. CTA — "Start Speaking Free" vs "Talk to Neha Ma'am Now"
3. Trial offer — "15 min free" vs "₹1 for 7 days"
4. Pricing — 3 tiers vs 2 tiers
5. Social proof placement — above vs below the fold

Run one test at a time. Do not test until each variant can collect ~100 conversions.

## 8. First 30 days — execution order

**Week 1 — stop the bleeding**
- Ship `index.html` at `/lp/speak`. Point all ads there. Never at the app.
- Strip the app nav from any page a logged-out user can reach.
- Move "15 min free, no signup" to the loudest position.
- Install GA4 + Google Ads conversion tags + Microsoft Clarity (free session recordings — you will
  learn more from 50 recordings than from any report).

**Week 2 — capture**
- ₹1 / 7-day offer live with UPI Autopay mandate.
- Post-session Fluency Score gate: score is shown, full report needs an account.
- WhatsApp opt-in checkbox at signup.

**Week 3 — retain**
- Streak + 3-minute daily goal + streak freeze.
- Mistake Book auto-populating from session transcripts.
- 8:30pm WhatsApp nudge.

**Week 4 — moat**
- Start logging L1 → error-pattern data deliberately, with a schema.
- Pitch Fluency Score to 10 BPO/staffing HR heads. Free pilot. Get one job posting to reference it.
- Ship audio-only low-bandwidth mode.

## 9. Metrics that matter

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LP → trial start | 20%+ | No-signup trial should convert hard |
| Trial → signup | 35%+ | Gate the score report |
| Signup → paid | 8–12% | Industry-realistic for freemium consumer edtech |
| D1 retention | 40%+ | Below 30% means the loop is broken — fix before scaling ads |
| D7 retention | 20%+ | The streak's job |
| D30 retention | 10%+ | The Mistake Book's job |
| CAC : LTV | 1 : 3 min | Do not scale ad spend until this holds |

**Do not scale Google Ads spend until D7 retention clears 20%.** Paid acquisition into a leaky
bucket is the single most common way Indian consumer edtech burns its runway.
