---
name: Rezi benchmark
description: Rezi is a product benchmark for resume tailoring and ATS workflows, not a runtime dependency for Lead Onto.
---

Use Rezi's public feature set as a benchmark: AI resume building, resume checking, keyword targeting, job-specific tailoring, cover letters, and interview preparation. Prefer implementing these workflows natively with Lead Onto's resume data, Firecrawl job extraction, and existing AI fallback rather than scraping or depending on Rezi.

**Why:** Lead Onto already owns the candidate profile, resume history, Indian-role context, interview practice, and job URL analysis; a direct Rezi integration would duplicate capabilities and add an external dependency without a demonstrated official application API.

**How to apply:** Prioritize a truthful job-specific resume rewrite and cover-letter flow after job matching; keep the user in Lead Onto and preserve resume version history.
