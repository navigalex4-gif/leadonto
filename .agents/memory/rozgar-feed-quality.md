---
name: Rozgar feed quality
description: Live Rozgar feeds need section-specific relevance filtering and source-grounded briefs.
---

Rozgar live feeds should filter headlines by section and candidate context before AI enrichment: remove overseas noise and duplicate titles, clean RSS descriptions, and pass readable title, summary, source, date, and link context to the editor. Briefs should use the Claude-first stream and a fixed structure with why it matters, takeaways, next move, and a 7-day plan.

**Why:** broad Google News queries can return unrelated or repetitive headlines, while Gemini quota retries made feed cards slow and generic.

**How to apply:** keep current facts source-backed, label general advice clearly, and never turn a news headline into a verified job. If no verified vacancy matches, show an honest empty state rather than inventing a listing.
