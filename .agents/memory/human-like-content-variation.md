---
name: Human-like content variation
description: Durable interaction rule for spontaneous, adaptive coaching and lesson generation across Lead Onto.
---

Live coaching and generative learning content should feel freshly authored for the current learner and visit. Use the learner's latest words to choose the next direction, vary openings and activity shapes, and allow short empathy/energy beats without repeating stock acknowledgements.

**Why:** Reuse and comeback value depends on learners feeling heard rather than being walked through a fixed questionnaire. Fixed fallback order and long-lived content caches make repeated sessions feel mechanical.

**How to apply:** Keep deterministic safety checks for repeated questions and session continuity, but shuffle fallback banks, pass a per-visit variation seed to generated lesson content, and prompt for varied reactions, pacing, difficulty, clarification, encouragement, and role-relevant follow-ups.
